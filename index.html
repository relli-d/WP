Caricatures

In Italy, especially in the northern regions, there is a longstanding tradition among university students. On the night of a graduation party, the graduate's friends gather to create a large poster. The poster usually includes phrases, anecdotes, photos, rhymes to complete, crosswords, and, most importantly, a large caricature of the graduate in the center. During the party, the poster is hung on a wall, and the graduate, dressed up in an embarassing costume and with a bottle of alcohol taped to their hand, must read it aloud and complete the games and challenges. Each time they make a mistake, they have to take a drink.
