Lu Free Spirit

Lu Free Spirit is an ongoing webcomic following a group of friends navigating creativity, friendship, and everyday chaos. Each episode is hand-drawn with an expressive line and a playful palette — part slice-of-life, part absurdist comedy. The series is published regularly on Instagram @lufreespirit.

Caricatures

A collection of commissioned caricature portraits created for graduation parties, birthdays, and private events.
